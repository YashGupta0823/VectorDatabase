1) I need to build an application around a persistent vector database for a programming assignment. I want something related to quantitative finance or trading that could also be useful in my portfolio. Suggest a practical application involving a substantial dataset, natural-language semantic search, runtime additions, and meaningful processing of retrieved results.

My top idea: AlphaSearch — AI-Powered Trading Research Finder
Concept: A semantic search engine for quantitative finance research. A user describes a trading idea, market concern, or investment strategy in everyday language, and the app finds relevant academic papers, explains how they're connected, and helps the user develop a research plan.

For example, someone could type:
"I want to protect my portfolio against sudden market crashes."

Instead of looking for those exact words, AlphaSearch could find research about tail-risk hedging, volatility derivatives, and downside protection.
